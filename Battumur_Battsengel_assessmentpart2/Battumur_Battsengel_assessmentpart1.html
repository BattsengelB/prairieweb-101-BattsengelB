About Kananaskis Trails

Kananaskis Country offers some of Alberta’s most scenic hiking routes. This simple website highlights a few challenging trails with rewarding views.

Popular Trails

Kananaskis Falls

Moderate · 2.3 km

Short, rugged, and unmaintained trail leading to a scenic canyon-enclosed waterfall.

Pocaterra Ridge

Hard · 10.1 km

A challenging trail with rewarding views of the surrounding mountains.

FAQ

Is this a real website?

No — it is a student project for a web development assessment. It may become a real website in the future, but for now it is just a demonstration of HTML and CSS skills.

Is the page responsive?

Yes. Bootstrap handles basic responsiveness automatically.

Where do the images of the trails come from?

The images are sourced from AllTrails, a popular trail discovery and social networking platform for hikers and outdoor enthusiasts.
